# BetterPvP
My current contract based job is with BetterPvP. They are Minecraft focused development team responsible for the current iteration of Clans and Champions, with the Mineplex brand.

Clans is an open-world class-based factions like gamemode with various mmo features (like bosses, dungeons, custom items). For those unaware of Minecraft factions, it is similar to games like Ark or Rust, where players team up in groups to build a base to defend and hold resources, while exploring the world to farm, mine, fish, etc. resources. The different groups fight to control these resources, attacking other bases to steal these resources in order to become the most powerful group.

Champions is a PvP object based game with different classes. Each class has a variety of skills to choose from, allowing players to mix and match different build sthat can drastically change how a class is played. There is currently 2 gamemodes, Domination, where 2 teams capture up to 5 control points on the map. All points controlled by a team earns points for that team every second. The first team to 10000 points wins. Capture the Flag, where 2 teams battle to capture the opposing flag. First to 3 wins, or whoever has the most after time runs out.
